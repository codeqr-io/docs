[CodeQR](https://codeqr.io) is the modern platform for creating and managing **dynamic QR Codes**. The image gets printed once — but your workflow creates the codes through the API, Make, or Zapier, you change where they point whenever you need to, and you see every scan.

But QR Codes are just the start. CodeQR also gives you the power to manage **dynamic short links**, build **personalized landing pages**, and measure every interaction with **real-time analytics**.

Whether you are:

- a developer generating QR Codes and links programmatically from your own app
- an automation builder wiring CodeQR into Make, Zapier, or Airtable
- a marketer launching QR-powered campaigns
- a creator or founder connecting an audience through QR and links

CodeQR helps you engage smarter, measure deeper, and grow faster.

## Key features

A generator hands you an image and walks away. With CodeQR the image is the only part that can’t change — **the destination, the data, and the automation behind it stay yours**.

Here’s what you can do with CodeQR:

- Create **dynamic QR Codes** that you can edit after printing
- Track scans with **real-time QR analytics**
- Build **personalized landing pages** for your QR campaigns
- Generate and manage **short links** ([bulk operations](/concepts/links/bulk-operations))
- Set up **conversion tracking** to measure impact
- Automate workflows with **Webhooks** and [integrations](https://codeqr.io/integrations)
- Access everything programmatically with our **REST API** and [native SDKs](/sdks/overview)
