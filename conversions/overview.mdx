# CodeQR Conversions Overview

Learn how you can use CodeQR Conversions to understand how your QR Code and short link clicks are converting into leads and revenue.

<Note>
  Conversion tracking requires a [Pro](https://codeqr.io/pricing) plan or higher (or an active conversion trial).
</Note>

Data is the lifeblood of any business. It helps you understand your customers, make data-driven decisions, and drive growth.

Whenever you run a marketing campaign, tracking link clicks alone isn't enough to paint a full picture of your customer acquisition funnel.

You need to track how those clicks are converting into signups and sales so you can determine which campaigns/channels are performing best.

**This is where CodeQR Conversions comes in.**

With CodeQR Conversions, you can turn any QR Code or short link you create on CodeQR into a complete attribution engine. This lets you understand how your links are translating into real users and revenue dollars.

## How does CodeQR Conversions work?

CodeQR Conversions turns your QR Codes, short links, and pages (forms) into powerful conversion-tracking tools. The process works in 3 main steps:

### 1. Creation and setup

First, you create a QR Code, short link, or page (form) on the CodeQR platform and enable conversion tracking:

- **QR Codes**: Ideal for offline campaigns, print materials, events, and packaging
- **Short Links**: Perfect for digital campaigns, email, social media, and SMS
- **Pages (Forms)**: A great option for capturing leads directly on the platform

### 2. User interaction

When someone interacts with your QR Code, link, or page, CodeQR automatically generates a unique identifier (`cq_id`) that is stored as a cookie in the user's browser:

- **QR Code scan**: The user scans the QR Code with their phone's camera
- **Short link click**: The user clicks the link in email, social media, or other channels
- **Page view**: The user visits a CodeQR page/form directly

  <img

  />

### 3. Conversion tracking

When the user performs a conversion action on your site or platform (signup, purchase, etc.), the system detects the `cq_id` cookie and attributes the conversion to the original QR Code, link, or page.

With this system, you can track the entire conversion funnel — from the first interaction to the final sale — and understand exactly which QR Code, link, or page is generating the most results.

## How are conversions tracked?

CodeQR Conversions works through a smart system of cookies and event tracking. Here's how it works in practice:

### Step 1: Generating the unique identifier (cq_id)

When a user interacts with your QR Code, short link, or CodeQR page, our system automatically generates a unique identifier (`cq_id`) that is passed as a parameter on the destination URL.

<Frame>
  <img
    className="rounded-lg border border-gray-100"
    src="https://assets.codeqr.io/help/conversion-click-event.png"
    alt="Diagram showing how the cq_id is generated and stored"
  />
</Frame>

**Examples of URLs with cq_id:**

- `yoursite.com?cq_id=abc123xyz`
- `store.com/product?cq_id=def456uvw`
- `app.example.com/signup?cq_id=ghi789rst`

### Step 2: Installing the analytics script

To capture and store the `cq_id`, you need to install our `@codeqr/analytics` script on your site or platform. You can do this in two ways:

**Option 1: Via CDN (simplest)**

```html
<script src="https://cdn.codeqr.io/analytics/script.js"></script>
```

**Option 2: Via NPM**

```bash
npm i @codeqr/analytics
```

This script automatically detects the `cq_id` parameter on the URL and stores it as a first-party cookie on your site's domain, letting you track conversions even as the user navigates across different pages.

### Step 3: Setting up conversion events

Now it's time to set up conversion tracking on your site or platform. **It's important to note that you, as the developer or site owner, must set up and call the events when a conversion action happens.**

When a user performs a conversion action (signup, purchase, download, etc.), you should:

1. **Check whether the `cq_id` cookie is present**
2. **Send the conversion event to CodeQR**
3. **Clear the cookie after sending**

<Frame>
  <img
    className="rounded-lg border border-gray-100"
    src="https://assets.codeqr.io/help/conversion-lead-event.png"
    alt="Diagram showing how conversion events are sent and processed"
  />
</Frame>

**Practical example — Tracking a signup (Lead):**

```typescript
// Example: When a user signs up on your site
import CodeQR from "@codeqr/ts";

const codeqr = new CodeQR({
  apiKey: process.env.CODEQR_API_KEY,
});

// Function called when a user signs up
async function handleUserSignup(userData) {
  // 1. Check whether the cq_id cookie exists
  const cq_id = getCookie("cq_id"); // function to read the cookie

  if (cq_id) {
    // 2. Send the lead event to CodeQR
    await codeqr.track.lead({
      clickId: cq_id,
      eventName: "Sign up",
      customerExternalId: userData.id,
      customerName: userData.name,
      customerEmail: userData.email,
    });

    // 3. Clear the cookie after sending
    deleteCookie("cq_id");
  }
}
```

**Practical example — Tracking a sale:**

```typescript
// Example: When a user makes a purchase on your site
async function handlePurchase(purchaseData) {
  // 1. Send the sale event to CodeQR
  await codeqr.track.sale({
    customerExternalId: purchaseData.customerId, // customer ID (same one used in the lead)
    amount: purchaseData.amount, // in the smallest unit of the currency (e.g. cents)
    paymentProcessor: "stripe", // or "custom" for other processors
    eventName: "Product purchase",
    currency: "usd",
    invoiceId: purchaseData.invoiceId, // invoice ID (optional)
  });
}
```

**Full conversion flow:**

1. **User scans a QR Code** → `cq_id` is generated and stored
2. **User signs up** → A **lead** event is sent with `cq_id`
3. **User makes a purchase** → A **sale** event is sent (without `cq_id`, since it was already cleared)
4. **CodeQR automatically connects** the sale to the original QR Code through the `customerExternalId`

When sending a lead event, you can include the following properties:

| Property             | Description                                                                                                          | Required |
| :------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------- |
| `clickId`            | The unique `cq_id` parameter that the lead conversion event is attributed to.                                        | **Yes**  |
| `eventName`          | The name of the event. Example: "Sign up".                                                                           | **Yes**  |
| `customerExternalId` | The unique ID of the customer in your system. Will be used to identify and attribute all future events to them.      | **Yes**  |
| `customerEmail`      | The email address of the customer.                                                                                   | No       |
| `customerName`       | The name of the customer. If not passed, a random name will be generated (e.g. "Big Red Caribou").                   | No       |
| `customerAvatar`     | The avatar URL of the customer.                                                                                      | No       |

The lead event serves as the source of truth for the customer's identity and which link they came from. This means that all subsequent actions the customer performs (e.g. upgrading their plan, buying a product) will automatically be attributed to the original link.

### Step 4: Viewing the data in the dashboard

Once tracking is set up, all conversion data automatically appears in your CodeQR Analytics and Events dashboard, where you can:

- **See real-time metrics**: Clicks/Scans, Leads, and Sales
- **Analyze the conversion funnel**: Understand where users drop off in the process
- **View individual events**: See each conversion with full details
- **Export reports**: Generate data for deeper analysis

## Integrations for sale tracking

To track sales, you can use our native integrations or implement it manually:

### Stripe integration (recommended)

Set it up in a few clicks and track sales automatically:

1. Install our Stripe integration in your CodeQR project
2. Set the `codeqrCustomerId` in the metadata of your checkout sessions
3. Sales are tracked automatically via webhooks

### Manual implementation

For other payment processors:

```typescript
// Example: Manual sale tracking
await codeqr.track.sale({
  customerExternalId: "customer_123",
  amount: 5900, // in the smallest unit of the currency (e.g. cents)
  paymentProcessor: "custom",
  eventName: "Product purchase",
  currency: "usd",
});
```

### 2. Tracking influencer campaign attribution

You can also use CodeQR Conversions to better understand the performance of your influencer campaigns — not just the number of clicks, but also the number of signups/purchases.

You can then use the data to make data-driven decisions about which influencers to work with and how to best allocate your budget to maximize your ROI.

CodeQR Conversions is especially powerful for campaigns that connect the physical world to the digital one through QR Codes:

- **QR Codes on print materials**: Track conversions from flyers, business cards, billboards, and banners
- **Events and trade shows**: Measure the impact of QR Codes on booths, promotional materials, and badges
- **Product packaging**: Understand how customers interact with QR Codes on products and packaging
- **Points of sale**: QR Codes on restaurant tables, storefronts, and promotional materials
- **Guerrilla marketing**: QR Codes on stickers, posters, and street materials

### 4. Digital campaigns with short links

Turn your short links into attribution tools:

- **Email marketing**: Track which email campaigns generate the most conversions
- **Social media**: Measure the ROI of posts on Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn
- **SMS and WhatsApp**: Understand the effectiveness of messaging campaigns
- **Influencers**: Track conversions from links shared by influencers

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to use CodeQR Conversions?

You need a [Pro](https://codeqr.io/pricing) plan or higher (or an active conversion trial) to access conversion tracking.

### Can I track conversions on existing links?

Yes! You can enable conversion tracking on existing links at any time through the Link Builder or the API.

### Does CodeQR Conversions work with static QR Codes?

Yes, CodeQR Conversions works with any type of QR Code created on the CodeQR platform, including static and dynamic QR Codes.

### Can I integrate with payment processors other than Stripe?

Yes! You can use our server-side SDKs to integrate with any payment processor or e-commerce system.

### Is conversion data secure?

Yes, all data is encrypted and stored securely. CodeQR complies with the major data privacy regulations.
